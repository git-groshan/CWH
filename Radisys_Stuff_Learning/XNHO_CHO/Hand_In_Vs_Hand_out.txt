In 5G (as in LTE), **handover** is the process of transferring an ongoing call or data session from one cell (gNB) to another. However, the terms **"hand\_in"** and **"hand\_out"** are informal but commonly used in logs, KPIs, and network engineering to describe the **direction of the handover**.

---

## 📘 Definitions:

### 🔁 **Hand-in** (Handover In)

* When a **UE enters** a cell **from another cell**.
* From the **serving cell's perspective**, the UE is coming **into** the cell.
* **Receiving side** of the handover.
* Used to count how many UEs are **handed over into** a gNB.

✅ Example:
UE is handed over **from Cell A → to Cell B**
For **Cell B**, this is a **hand-in**

---

### 🔁 **Hand-out** (Handover Out)

* When a **UE leaves** a cell and is handed over to a **target cell**.
* From the **serving cell's perspective**, the UE is going **out**.
* **Sending side** of the handover.
* Used to count how many UEs are **handed over from** a gNB.

✅ Example:
UE is handed over **from Cell A → to Cell B**
For **Cell A**, this is a **hand-out**

---

## 📊 Why it's important

Network teams track **hand-in and hand-out KPIs** to measure:

* Cell performance
* Handover success rates
* Mobility imbalance (if too many hand-outs and fewer hand-ins)

Example KPI names:

* `HO_Attempts_Out`
* `HO_Success_In`
* `Inter-gNB_Handover_HandIn_Success`

---

## 🧠 In 5G (NG-RAN)

* **Handover** can be:

  * **Intra-gNB** (within same base station)
  * **Inter-gNB** (between different gNBs)
  * **Inter-RAT** (to/from LTE)

* In signaling:

  * `Handover Request` → from source to target
  * `Handover Request Acknowledge` → from target to source
  * UE switches cells based on command

---

## 🔧 Example Scenario:

UE is connected to **gNB A** and moving toward **gNB B**.

| Step | Event                  | Perspective             |
| ---- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- |
| 1️⃣  | UE moving out of gNB A | **Hand-out** from gNB A |
| 2️⃣  | UE entering gNB B      | **Hand-in** to gNB B    |
